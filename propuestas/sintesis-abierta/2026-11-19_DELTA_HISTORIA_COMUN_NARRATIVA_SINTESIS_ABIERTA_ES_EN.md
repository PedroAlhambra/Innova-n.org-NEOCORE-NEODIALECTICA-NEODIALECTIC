## ES · Alcance y novedad

La petición originaria de Neo0 propone que la construcción de la narrativa histórica sea conjunta mediante Síntesis Abierta para reducir la captura de la memoria colectiva por actores con poder sobre archivo, educación o distribución.

- Neoaxiomas: NAX-06 (Memoria de Ausencia), NAX-22 (Memoria Material-Relacional), NAX-24 (Diagnóstico ≠ Síntesis), NAX-25 (Fenómeno ≠ Narrativa), NAX-26 (Poder/Trazabilidad y Acusación/Evidencia), NAX-27 (Soberanía Diferenciada), NAX-29 (Federabilidad sin Absorción).

**Diferencial candidato:** pasar de custodiar la memoria y separar fenómeno de narrativa a un contrato de *reconstrucción histórica conjunta, probatoriamente asimétrica, versionable, federable y reabrible*. Incluye qué se mantiene, cómo se atribuyen aportes, cómo se contrastan fuentes, cómo se registra disenso y qué impide coronar un nuevo relato dominante.

**Antítesis y falsadores necesarios:** ¿basta un corolario de NAX-06/25/26/27 sin nuevo neoaxioma? ¿Cómo se evita que la apertura amplifique negacionismos, propaganda, doxxing o fabricación de pruebas? ¿Cómo se gestionan archivos restringidos, memoria traumática y desigualdad de recursos? Ninguna acusación de captura concreta se eleva a hecho sin evidencia.

**Decisión documental actual:** candidato de manifiesto sin numeración canónica (LXXXVII existe como trabajo local no confirmado en otra copia, mientras la rama remota leída sigue hasta LXXXVI); C-NAX-31 candidato, sin atribuir fijación. ## EN · Scope and novelty

Neo0's originating request proposes jointly constructing the historical narrative through Open Synthesis, reducing the possibility of collective memory being captured by actors with disproportionate control over archives, educational channels, communication infrastructure, or distribution. It does not claim that every established account is false or that every excluded account is true. Narrative capture in a specific case is a proposition to investigate, not a conclusion inferred from who speaks.

**Recovered framework, not recreated:**

- RO AX-NEO0-07 (SAN), 09 (Common Good), 12 (Memory), 13 (Continuity), 14 (Append-Only), 20 (Delta), 22 (Relations), 23 (Layers), 37 (Total Reading), 38 (Event-Reflection), 56 (Self-Correction), 68 (Non-Retroactivity), 71 (Human Priority), 89 (Culture), and 100–102 (Axiomatic Tree and Rereading).
- RO: the R9 SAN contract; the R11 S4 INIT contract distinguishing consensus from truth, candidates from canon, and a model's knowledge gaps from gaps in the framework.
- Public manifestos: II (SAN), IX (Memory/Genealogy/Traceability), XIX (Persistence), XLVI (Reconciliation), LI (Civic Power), LVIII (Civilisational Intelligence), LXXVII (Phenomenon/Narrative), LXXXIII (Circulation of Truth), LXXXIV (Access to Power), and LXXXVI (Network Federation).
- Neoaxioms: NAX-06 (Memory of Absence), NAX-22 (Material-Relational Memory), NAX-24 (Diagnosis ≠ Synthesis), NAX-25 (Phenomenon ≠ Narrative), NAX-26 (Power/Traceability and Accusation/Evidence), NAX-27 (Differentiated Sovereignty), and NAX-29 (Federability without Absorption).

**Candidate increment:** move from preserving memory and separating event from narrative toward a *jointly reconstructible, evidence-weighted, versioned, federable, and reopenable historical account*. The proposed procedure records how testimony and documents enter, how assertions are assessed, how evidence is attributed, how dissent is preserved, and how new synthesis explains its differences from earlier interpretations. It rejects both a dominant monopoly on narrative and the false balance that grants equal evidential standing to every claim.

**Counterarguments and falsifiers:** Could this principle remain a corollary of NAX-06/25/26/27 without a separate Neoaxiom? How can openness prevent amplifying denial of well-documented atrocities, propaganda, harassment, exposure of protected personal information, or fabricated evidence? What rules govern restricted archives, traumatic memory, consent, and unequal access to resources? No specific accusation of narrative capture becomes factual without its own evidence.

**Current documentary decision:** an unnumbered candidate manifesto, because LXXXVII exists as an uncommitted document in another local worktree while the fetched remote main still ends at LXXXVI; candidate C-NAX-31 only, outside the canonical registration path. No changes to RO, no promotion, no change to the finite manifesto frontier, and no assertion that a shared-history WEB4 application is deployed.
