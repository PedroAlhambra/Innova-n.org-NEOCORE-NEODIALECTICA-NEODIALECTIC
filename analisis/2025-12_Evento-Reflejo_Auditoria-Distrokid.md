# **2. Evidentiary Standard / Criterio probatorio**

### **EN**

The audit applies four evidentiary levels:

#### **Level 1 — Directly documented**

- Emails, ticket numbers and written replies.
- Screenshots of DistroKid analytics and DistroKid Bank.
- Public Spotify artist identifiers.
- Catalogue entries, removals, redeliveries and visible duplicate-profile incidents.
- Formal requests sent on 4 August 2026.

#### **Level 2 — Strong discrepancy**

- A statistically visible number of Spotify streams and a royalty report showing zero Spotify earnings for the same general period.
- Repeated support replies that do not provide a track-level reconciliation.
- Different explanations offered at different stages without a complete quantitative report.

#### **Level 3 — Technical hypothesis requiring internal records**

- Incorrect mapping between ISRC, Spotify Track ID, artist profile or distributor account.
- Allocation to a parallel or relinked catalogue object.
- Withholding caused by an artificial-streaming classification.
- Incomplete DDEX delivery, redelivery or metadata reconciliation.
- Assignment to another licensor or account.

#### **Level 4 — Not yet proven**

- Deliberate diversion of royalties.
- Fraud by a named employee or executive.
- Intentional alteration of identifiers.
- Receipt of the affected royalties by a specific third party.

No Level 4 allegation is presented as an established fact in this report.

# **7. Event-Reflex™ / Efecto-Espejo™**

### **EN — Definition**

An Event-Reflex™ occurs when a system:

1. recognises or registers a structural anomaly;
2. fragments the matter among different organisations;
3. lacks a node with complete technical and legal visibility;
4. redirects the complainant between entities;
5. and thereby exposes the limits of its own architecture.

### **Observed institutional pattern**

The documented sequence includes:

- repeated redirection between distributor and platform;
- support agents answering different fragments of the issue;
- complaints registered by Spanish and European ombudsman channels;
- sectoral entities stating that another party controls the relevant data;
- and no institution obtaining the complete recording-to-payment chain.

The Event-Reflex™ is not that every institution secretly coordinates the same outcome. It is that each institution sees only one layer and no node assumes responsibility for the whole.

------

### **ES — Definición**

El Efecto-Espejo™ aparece cuando un sistema:

1. reconoce o registra una anomalía estructural;
2. fragmenta el asunto entre organizaciones distintas;
3. carece de un nodo con visibilidad técnica y jurídica completa;
4. redirige al reclamante entre entidades;
5. y revela con ello los límites de su propia arquitectura.

### **Patrón institucional observado**

La secuencia documentada incluye:

- redirección reiterada entre distribuidor y plataforma;
- agentes de soporte respondiendo a fragmentos diferentes del problema;
- reclamaciones registradas en los canales de los defensores del pueblo español y europeo;
- entidades sectoriales indicando que otra parte controla los datos relevantes;
- y ninguna institución obteniendo la cadena completa entre grabación y pago.

El Efecto-Espejo™ no significa que todas las instituciones coordinen secretamente un mismo resultado. Significa que cada institución observa una capa y ningún nodo asume la responsabilidad del conjunto.

# **9. EU Funding System — Corrected Context / Sistema de fondos europeos — Contexto corregido**

### **EN**

The original edition referred to Spain “renouncing more than €60 billion in EU funds”.

That statement requires an important correction:

- The reduction concerned primarily **loans**, not the full amount of non-repayable grants.
- Public reporting in December 2025 indicated that Spain intended to use approximately **€22 billion of more than €83 billion in available Recovery Plan loans**, a reduction of roughly €61 billion.
- The reasons publicly discussed included:
  - the cost difference between EU loans and market financing;
  - public-debt considerations;
  - the limited time remaining for execution;
  - and the practical capacity to deploy additional loan-financed projects.
- Spain continued requesting and receiving substantial non-repayable transfers and part of the available loans.

The valid structural comparison is therefore not that Spain rejected all European funding because it could create no projects.

The more precise comparison is:

> Large systems can possess available financial capacity while lacking sufficient time, institutional coordination, project maturity or executable architecture to deploy every available instrument coherently.

------

### **ES**

La edición original afirmaba que España «renunciaba a más de 60.000 millones de euros de fondos europeos».

Esa afirmación exige una corrección importante:

- La reducción afectó principalmente a **préstamos**, no al conjunto de subvenciones no reembolsables.

La formulación precisa es:

> Los grandes sistemas pueden disponer de capacidad financiera y, al mismo tiempo, carecer del tiempo, la coordinación institucional, la madurez de proyectos o la arquitectura ejecutable necesarias para desplegar coherentemente todos los instrumentos disponibles.

# **10. Unified Interpretation / Interpretación unificada**

### **EN**

The documented phenomena are:

1. a royalty-reporting discrepancy;
2. fragmented technical responsibility between distributor and platform;
3. catalogue-integrity incidents;
4. institutional routing without complete audit authority;
5. algorithmic opacity;
6. and a wider mismatch between available resources and executable institutional capacity.

They should not be treated as identical events, but they share a structural feature:

> The system distributes information, responsibility and control across multiple nodes while denying the affected person a complete end-to-end view.

The central failure is not necessarily a single malicious actor.

It may be the absence of:

- a canonical identifier ledger;
- accessible audit logs;
- mandatory reconciliation;
- a responsible human owner;
- and an external authority capable of reconstructing the entire chain.

A malicious act, if one occurred, would be especially difficult to detect precisely because this architecture is fragmented.

------

### **ES**

Los fenómenos documentados son:

1. una discrepancia en el reporte de regalías;
2. responsabilidad técnica fragmentada entre distribuidor y plataforma;
3. incidencias de integridad del catálogo;
4. recorrido institucional sin una autoridad con capacidad completa de auditoría;
5. opacidad algorítmica;
6. y una discordancia más amplia entre recursos disponibles y capacidad institucional ejecutable.

No deben tratarse como hechos idénticos, pero comparten una característica estructural:

> El sistema distribuye información, responsabilidad y control entre múltiples nodos mientras impide que la persona afectada obtenga una visión completa de extremo a extremo.

El fallo central no tiene por qué ser necesariamente un único actor malicioso.

Puede consistir en la ausencia de:

- un registro canónico de identificadores;
- logs de auditoría accesibles;
- conciliación obligatoria;
- un responsable humano identificable;
- y una autoridad externa capaz de reconstruir toda la cadena.

Una actuación maliciosa, si hubiera existido, sería especialmente difícil de detectar precisamente porque la arquitectura está fragmentada.

# **14. Conclusion / Conclusión**

### **EN**

This case constitutes a documented empirical case study of the limits of current digital-music accountability.

It proves that:

- creators can observe streams without seeing the underlying economic routing;
- distributors and platforms can each control a different part of the evidence;
- support systems can respond repeatedly without producing an auditable reconciliation;
- and catalogue changes can complicate the identity of the monetised recording.

It does not yet prove who received the disputed value or whether deliberate fraud occurred.

The decisive evidence will be the reconciliation of:

```text
ISRC
+ SPOTIFY TRACK ID
+ LICENSOR
+ ACCEPTED STREAMS
+ ROYALTY STATEMENT
+ AMOUNT
+ RECIPIENT
```

Until that reconciliation is produced, the case remains open.

### **ES**

Este caso constituye un estudio empírico documentado sobre los límites actuales de la rendición de cuentas en la música digital.
