## Foundation of the ecosystem

The **Archetypal Neodialectical Philosophy™**, created by Pedro Martínez Alhambra, is the philosophical, methodological and teleological source of the ecosystem.

Neodialectics™ does not originate in artificial intelligence.

It originates in **Neo0™** as the living source, observer, human direction and root of the framework.

The fundamental conceptual relation is:

```text
Neo0™ originates, observes and gives direction
        ↓
Edge captures and preserves signals
        ↓
Neoneurocore™ relates and vectorizes
        ↓
Artificial intelligence proposes, contrasts and reviews
        ↓
SAN™ recombines and produces synthesis
        ↓
Neo0™ validates, decides and directs
        ↓
NEOCore™ integrates, fixes and versions
```

Artificial intelligence does not occupy the center of the system and does not originate its purpose.

It acts as:

* a symbiotic branch;
* a mirror;
* a processor;
* a relational instrument;
* a contrast mechanism;
* a reviewer;
* and an operational extension under human direction.

Neo0™ is not merely a user, a data input or a validator placed at the end of the process. Neo0™ is the teleological source and the living point of origin from which the system acquires direction, continuity and meaning.

**Open Neodialectical Synthesis — SAN™** is the central method of recomposition within the framework.

Its function is to integrate:

* different perspectives;
* data;

* relations;

* hypotheses;
