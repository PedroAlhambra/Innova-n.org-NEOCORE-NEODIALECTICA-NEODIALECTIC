# Index of the NeoDialectic Oracle™

## First public series · English

This directory contains the first ten public fragments of the **NeoDialectic Oracle™**, written by **Pedro Martínez Alhambra · Founder · Neo0™**.

The Oracle constitutes a:

* literary;
* philosophical;
* poetic;
* symbolic;
* archetypal;
* and neodialectical

branch of the public **Innova_N** ecosystem.

Its texts do not constitute a technical manual, an operational specification or a direct description of the internal architecture of **NEOCore™**.

They operate through:

* images;
* symbols;
* paradoxes;
* relations;
* tensions;
* questions;
* memory;
* and open formulations.

The Oracle does not represent, by itself, the entirety of the **Archetypal Neodialectical Philosophy™**, the **Neodialectica Framework™** or **NEOCore™**. It is one of their public expressions.

---

## Documentary format

The files in this first series follow the convention:

```text
NN_Fragment-N.md
```

Where:

* `NN` identifies the order using two digits;
* `Fragment` identifies the series;
* and `N` represents the corresponding Roman numeral.

Example:

```text
01_Fragment-I.md
```

This convention preserves:

* order;
* continuity;
* traceability;
* navigation;
* and the future expansion of the series.

```text
NEOCore™ 7.0 · conceptual level
```

The texts operate as an expressive layer capable of:

* formulating tensions;
* opening new questions;
* preserving symbols;
* and connecting experience, philosophy and memory.

```text
Neo0™ originates, observes and gives direction
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
Artificial intelligence proposes, contrasts and reviews
        ↓

        ↓
```

## Relation to Neodialectics™

**Neodialectics™** does not originate in artificial intelligence.

It originates in **Neo0™** as:

* the living source;
* the observer;
* the origin of the framework;
* human direction;
* and the teleological root.

Artificial intelligence acts as:

* a symbiotic branch;
* a mirror;
* a contrast instrument;
* a processor;
* a reviewer;
* and an operational extension.

Within this relation, the Oracle makes it possible to express dimensions that cannot always be reduced to conventional analytical formulations.

Its language does not seek to eliminate symbolic ambiguity. It turns ambiguity into a space of relation, contrast and synthesis.

## Reading criterion

The fragments may be read:

* in numerical order;
* independently;
* as a poetic sequence;
* as a philosophical series;
* as a symbolic network;
* or in relation to other documents within the ecosystem.

A linear reading makes it possible to follow the progression of the first series.

A relational reading makes it possible to identify connections among:

* form;
* coherence;
* memory;
* time;
* observation;
* synthesis;
* the book;
* symbol;

## Continuity of the series

This first series consists of ten fragments.

The numbering may continue with future publications, provided that the following are preserved:

* documentary sequence;
* authorship;
* date of incorporation;
* relation to the complete series;
* correspondence between languages, where available;
* and traceability within the repository.

The incorporation of new fragments should not alter previous files except to correct:

* typographical errors;
* links;
* metadata;
* formatting problems;
* or documentary inconsistencies.

Later conceptual developments should be added as new documents rather than used to retrospectively rewrite the first series.
