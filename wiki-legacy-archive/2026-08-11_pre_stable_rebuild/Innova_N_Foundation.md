# ES

## 1. Naturaleza y estado

La **Fundación Innova_N** es una propuesta institucional destinada a dar continuidad pública, jurídica y organizativa al ecosistema Innova_N.

Actualmente no debe presentarse como:

- entidad jurídicamente constituida;
- organización operativa completa;
- estructura con personal contratado;
- titular automática de los derechos del ecosistema;
- organización financiada;
- ni institución territorial desplegada.

```text
Fundación Innova_N
=
propuesta institucional
+ dirección futura
+ desarrollo progresivo
```

La autoría, dirección y titularidad presentes corresponden a **Pedro Martínez Alhambra · Neo0™**, salvo transferencia jurídica futura formalmente documentada.

---

## 2. Misión

La misión propuesta es impulsar proyectos orientados al **Bien Común** mediante la relación entre:

- filosofía;
- inteligencia humana y artificial;
- conocimiento;
- cultura;
- educación;
- tecnología;
- sostenibilidad;
- memoria;
- y territorio.

La Fundación se concibe como posible soporte para:

- investigación;
- documentación;
- creación cultural;
- formación;
- colaboración;
- desarrollo tecnológico;
- proyectos sociales;
- y experimentación institucional responsable.

No sustituye la Filosofía Arquetípica Neodialéctica™, el Framework/Network, NEOCore™, NAVE™ o WEB4™.

Su función futura sería ofrecer una forma institucional compatible con ellos.

---

## 3. Principios

La propuesta se orienta mediante:

- dirección humana;
- dignidad;
- responsabilidad;
- trazabilidad;
- transparencia proporcional;
- autonomía;
- participación;
- sostenibilidad;
- capacidad de corrección;
- y protección de la autoría y la memoria.

La tecnología debe mantenerse subordinada a la finalidad humana y al Bien Común.

```text
capacidad técnica
+ responsabilidad
+ memoria
+ participación
=
institución corregible
```

La Fundación no se plantea como estructura cerrada ni como autoridad incuestionable.

---

## 4. Áreas de desarrollo

Las áreas públicas pueden agruparse en cinco líneas:

| Área | Alcance |
|---|---|
| **Conocimiento y educación** | Investigación, aprendizaje, documentación y transferencia |
| **Cultura y creación** | Literatura, música, memoria, patrimonio y producción cultural |
| **Tecnología e IA** | Herramientas locales, trazables y orientadas al Bien Común |
| **Sociedad y gobernanza** | Participación, sistemas humanos y experimentación institucional |
| **Territorio y sostenibilidad** | Medio ambiente, resiliencia, hábitat y desarrollo local |

Estas áreas expresan dirección.

No implican que existan actualmente programas, equipos, presupuestos o infraestructuras desplegadas en todas ellas.

# EN

## 1. Nature and status

The **Innova_N Foundation** is an institutional proposal intended to provide public, legal and organisational continuity to the Innova_N ecosystem.

It should not currently be presented as:

- a legally constituted entity;
- a complete operating organisation;
- a structure with employed staff;
- the automatic rights holder of the ecosystem;
- a funded organisation;
- or a territorially deployed institution.

```text
Innova_N Foundation
=
institutional proposal
+ future direction
+ progressive development
```

Present authorship, direction and ownership remain with **Pedro Martínez Alhambra · Neo0™**, unless a future legal transfer is formally documented.

---

## 2. Mission

The proposed mission is to promote projects oriented towards the **Common Good** through the relationship among:

- philosophy;
- human and artificial intelligence;
- knowledge;
- culture;
- education;
- technology;
- sustainability;
- memory;
- and territory.

The Foundation is conceived as possible support for:

- research;
- documentation;
- cultural creation;
- education;
- collaboration;
- technological development;
- social projects;
- and responsible institutional experimentation.

It does not replace the Archetypal Neodialectical Philosophy™, the Framework/Network, NEOCore™, NAVE™ or WEB4™.

Its future role would be to provide an institutional form compatible with them.

---

## 3. Principles

The proposal is oriented by:

- human direction;
- dignity;
- responsibility;
- traceability;
- proportional transparency;
- autonomy;
- participation;
- sustainability;
- capacity for correction;
- and protection of authorship and memory.

Technology should remain subordinate to human purpose and the Common Good.

```text
technical capability
+ responsibility
+ memory
+ participation
=
correctable institution
```

The Foundation is not conceived as a closed structure or unquestionable authority.

---

## 4. Areas of development

The public areas may be grouped into five lines:

| Area | Scope |
|---|---|
| **Knowledge and education** | Research, learning, documentation and transfer |
| **Culture and creation** | Literature, music, memory, heritage and cultural production |
| **Technology and AI** | Local, traceable tools oriented towards the Common Good |
| **Society and governance** | Participation, human systems and institutional experimentation |
| **Territory and sustainability** | Environment, resilience, habitat and local development |

These areas express direction.

They do not imply that programmes, teams, budgets or infrastructures are currently deployed in all of them.

---

## 5. Territorial vision

**Val de Santo Domingo** forms part of the future vision as a possible territory for experimentation and development.

The imagined direction includes:

- Smart Village;
- living lab;
- local Edge;
- reforestation;
- environmental robotics;
- housing rehabilitation;
- heritage recovery;
- and qualified repopulation.

This vision belongs to the roadmap.

It should not be presented as:

- an active campus;
- a constituted foundation headquarters;
- deployed infrastructure;
- a funded project;
- or an ongoing territorial operation.

```text
territorial vision
≠
current deployment
```

---

## 6. Institutional development and public limits

Future constitution would require, among other elements:

- legal form;
- statutes;
- board or governing body;
- registered office;
- resources;
- accounting;
- regulatory compliance;
- intellectual-property policy;
- data protection;
- and participation and oversight mechanisms.

| Dimension | Status |
|---|---|
| Vision and mission | Defined |
| Conceptual framework | Active |
| Legal entity | Not constituted |
| Final statutes | Not published |
| Governing bodies | Not constituted |
| Staff and budget | Not declared |
| Stable public programmes | Not deployed |
| Territorial vision | Future roadmap |

This page does not publish internal legal drafts, personal data, budgets, agreements, operational architecture or private procedures.

The use of “Innova_N Foundation” expresses a future institutional direction, not an already acquired legal personality.
